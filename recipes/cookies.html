Chocolate Chip Cookies


Description

Bake a batch of classic, bakery-style chocolate chip cookies that are crispy on the outside and chewy on the inside. This foolproof recipe guarantees perfect results every time.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Chocolate Chips
  • Baking Soda and Vanilla

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients and preheat your kitchen oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).
  2. Cream the softened butter, white sugar, and brown sugar together in a large mixing bowl until smooth and fluffy.
  3. Beat the eggs into the mixture one at a time. Stir in the vanilla extract, baking soda, and salt until fully incorporated.
  4. Gradually mix in the flour until a soft dough forms. Fold in the chocolate chips evenly using a spatula.
  5. Drop rounded spoonfuls of dough onto ungreased baking sheets. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes until edges are golden. Let cool.
