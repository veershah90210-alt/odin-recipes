Chicken Alfredo


Description

Enjoy a restaurant-quality meal at home with this rich, creamy chicken alfredo. It is an incredibly simple dish that requires minimal effort and delivers maximum comfort.

Ingredients

  • Chicken Breast
  • Fettuccine Noodles
  • Heavy Cream
  • Butter
  • Parmesan Cheese
  • Garlic and Seasoning

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients and bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a rolling boil.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Season chicken breasts and cook until golden brown and no longer pink, about 6 to 8 minutes per side. Slice into strips.
  3. Boil the fettuccine noodles in the pot of water for 8 to 10 minutes until tender. Drain thoroughly.
  4. Melt butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Stir in heavy cream, minced garlic, and grated Parmesan cheese; simmer gently for 5 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  5. Toss the drained noodles and sliced chicken directly into the warm alfredo sauce. Garnish with parsley and fresh black pepper before serving.
